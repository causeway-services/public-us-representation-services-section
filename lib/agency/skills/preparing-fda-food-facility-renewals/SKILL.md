# Prepare FDA food facility renewals — actual customer materials

This is a [Practical Skill](https://github.com/561-group/561-group-services-section/blob/main/content/text/glossary/terms/practical-skill.md), deliberately distinct from the cross-domain [Modernize-and-Formalize Tactical Skill](https://github.com/coesite-operations/operations-services-section/blob/main/lib/agency/skills/modernize-and-formalize/SKILL.md). Source owner is the existing Causeway U.S. Representation service; its native operations use the original AOE enterprise journal and Coesite methods. This skill neither creates an FDA representative, provides agency authority, nor substitutes a separate registration engine.

**The client job:** prepare a truthful and useful registration-renewal brief from the facility's own declared facts, in English and Chinese, using FDA's 2026 program. An actual food facility must decide registration applicability and submit (or separately authorize an individual to submit) to the FDA's own Food Facility Registration Module. Independent paid assistance is **optional**, and FDA government registration/renewal fee is **$0**. The selected contract must never claim an FDA official affiliation or a medical-device registration.

1. **Serve free public guidance first.** With non-secret facility declarations (domestic/foreign, whether required to register, whether registered before October 1, 2026, ownership change, recognized UFI, qualified-facility attestation, authorized submitter, and foreign U.S.-agent status), call the actual `representation-food-renewal-brief` operation or its original `@red-cup-engineering/us-representation-services-section/food-renewal-preparation` pure export. It emits one bilingual Markdown customer brief, official references, a dated state and unresolved source/customer questions. It makes **no filing**. Do not seek a client's FDA account password.
2. **Quote only a scoped optional private service.** When the actual client wants additional paid preparation, open the original `representation-engagement-record offer=food-renewal-preparation stage=proposed fdaProgram=food-facility-registration-biennial-renewal` for a specific facility and a real authorized enterprise counterparty. Read the actual proposal and intended deliverable, then use `representation-document-draft template=food-renewal-preparation-agreement engagement=<original order>`. Do not send, sign or charge without the original customer authority; the template is a **draft**.
3. **Do the contracted Work through the original customer relationship.** Only an actual formed and permitted contracting principal with a signed service agreement, scoped client, country and independently checked sanctions standing may move the record to `signed`. Record the separately agreed `preparationFee`, not a fictitious FDA fee. `active` requires documented authorization for processing the client's information, and the work remains preparation only. Deliver the actual brief and any agreed work product; `closed` requires source-referenced customer acceptance. One-off preparation is not the ongoing `us-agent` commercial product and cannot pass through an invented annual renewal stage.
4. **Keep agency and submitted filing separate.** An independently appointed foreign-facility U.S. agent requires physical U.S. presence, real communication coverage, signed and accepted appointment and separate liability terms. The food-preparation agreement does not confer these. Registrations and qualified-facility attestations are FDA account effects, not effects of reading a checklist. Any external portal action requires its own authorized Practical Skill and evidence of actually returned FDA results.
5. **Receive actual value.** The source-built brief is a useful potential customer deliverable; only the original client accepting it is delivery. A prospect is not a payer, a signed contract is not booked cash, and an arbitrary payment number is not a verified bank settlement. Apply the Opportunity Services four gates and measure honest after-cost contribution if externally paid. Restore the source owner on any observed failure.

**Native consumer:** `usRepresentationOperations` exports `representation-food-renewal-brief` and the existing engagement, document, CRM and payment operations. The pure helper's client input and refusal tests are `red-cup-engineering/us-representation-engineering-services-section/test/food-renewal-preparation.test.mjs`; run `npm test` in `red-cup-engineering/us-representation-engineering-services-section` with the installed package closure. The customer-facing practical skill remains **source-only** unless the live MetroPlat operations catalog advertises and lawfully executes the exact released consumer.

**Three ways to exercise it:** (A) a qualified domestic facility renewal during October–December 2026; (B) a different foreign facility with an independently designated U.S. agent and any applicable qualified-facility attestation; (C) refusal or scope-review for changed ownership, missing UFI, unknown applicability, unauthorized submitter, unsupported customer claim, missing agent, closed period, or false FDA-government service charge. None can be honestly transformed into FDA submission evidence by this operation.
