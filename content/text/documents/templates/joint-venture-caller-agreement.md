# US Representation Joint-Venture Caller Agreement

**Between:** {{contractingParty}} ("the Group") and ______________________ ("the Caller").

1. **The venture.** The Group and the Caller jointly pursue US representation engagements with
   foreign makers: holding FDA US agent roles, selling makers' lines to US buyers on commission,
   and local presence work. Each engagement the Caller originates is a joint venture between
   them for that engagement.
2. **Origination.** An engagement is the Caller's when the Caller first logs a contact telling
   for the client's interest (interested, callback, engagement sent) before the engagement is
   recorded.
3. **Roles.** The Caller reaches makers, qualifies them and gets their agreement to terms. The
   Group screens the client, signs the agreement, and does the work.
4. **Split.** For each engagement the Caller originates, the Caller receives {{callerPercent}}%
   of every payment the Group receives on it in the engagement's first year, paid within five
   business days after the Group receives it.
5. **Expenses.** Each party pays its own phone, time and tools.
6. **Independence.** The Caller works their own hours with their own means, is not an
   employee, and is responsible for their own taxes. The Group issues a 1099 where required.
7. **Conduct.** The Caller identifies themselves and the Group truthfully, quotes only the
   terms the Group has set, honors every request not to be contacted again, and does not sign
   for the Group.
8. **Leads.** The establishment lists and the contacts the Caller develops are shared venture
   property. Either party may end this agreement by written notice; engagements signed before
   then are still split under section 4.
