# Sales Representative Agreement / 销售代表协议

**Date:** {{date}}

**Between:** {{client}} ("the Maker") and {{contractingParty}} ("the Representative").

1. **Appointment.** The Maker appoints the Representative as its independent sales
   representative to solicit orders for {{products}} ("the Products") from buyers in
   {{territory}} ("the Territory"). The appointment is non-exclusive unless the parties sign an
   exclusivity schedule.
2. **What the Representative does.** Finds and calls on buyers in the Territory, presents the
   Products, relays quotes and orders to the Maker, follows up on delivery and service
   questions in English in US hours, and attends trade shows the parties agree on.
3. **Orders.** The Representative does not accept orders or bind the Maker. Buyers order from
   and pay the Maker, and the Maker ships and invoices. The Representative takes no title to
   the Products.
4. **Commission.** {{commissionPercent}}% of the net invoice value (excluding freight, duties
   and taxes) of every order from a buyer in the Territory, whether or not the Representative
   took the order, paid monthly within fifteen days after the end of the month in which the
   Maker is paid. The Maker sends a statement of orders, invoices and payments with each
   commission payment.
5. **Commercial scope.** This engagement is private and nonpolitical commercial service in
   furtherance of the Maker's trade: {{commercialScope}}. The Representative does not lobby
   or carry out political or public-opinion work for the Maker.
6. **Independence.** The Representative is an independent contractor, pays its own expenses
   unless the parties agree otherwise in writing, and may represent other makers whose products
   do not compete with the Products.
7. **Ending.** Under Minnesota Statutes section 325E.37, the Maker may end this agreement only
   for good cause, on ninety days' written notice stating the reasons, with sixty days for the
   Representative to cure, except for the causes that statute makes immediate. The
   Representative may end it on thirty days' written notice. On any ending the Maker pays
   commission on every order it received before the agreement ended or the notice period ran
   out, whichever is later, whether shipped before or after, within the time Minnesota Statutes
   section 181.145 sets.
8. **Screening.** The Maker confirms that neither it nor any owner of 25% or more is on a US
   government restricted-party list; the Representative may end this agreement at once if one
   is.
9. **Law.** Minnesota law governs this agreement. The English text controls.
