## 1. Independent, optional service / 独立、自愿的服务

The Client may register and renew **directly with FDA at no government registration or renewal fee**, without hiring us or another third-party registrar. We are **not FDA, affiliated with FDA, or authorized to speak on FDA's behalf**. The Client purchases only the independently described administrative preparation and document-review service below.

客户可直接向 FDA **免费**注册或续期，无需聘请第三方注册代理。服务方**不是 FDA 或其关联机构**，亦不代表 FDA 发言。本协议仅涉及下列自愿购买的行政准备和材料核对服务。

Official government source: https://www.fda.gov/food/guidance-regulation-food-and-dietary-supplements/questions-regarding-whether-food-facilities-are-required-pay-registration-fees-and-private

## 2. Deliverable, not government filing / 交付材料不等于提交申请

The agreed deliverable is a source-cited checklist and readiness brief covering applicable facility-registration requirements, the declared facility record and ownership, recognized unique facility identifier (UFI), qualified-facility attestation questions if applicable, foreign-facility U.S.-agent details when relevant, and instructions for the Client's **own** FDA Industry Systems Food Facility Registration Module (FFRM) submission. Original client information is not declared accurate merely because we reviewed it.

**The Client or an individually and expressly authorized submitter makes the actual submission**, confirms the truth of the declarations, and retains the official FDA confirmation. This agreement does not authorize us to enter the Client's FDA account or submit a filing. No account password, MFA secret or privileged portal session shall be delivered to our systems. If a separate filing service is later negotiated, it needs a distinct customer authorization and admitted practical operation; it is not silently included.

交付物为有官方来源的核对清单和准备说明，**并非 FDA 的正式提交或确认**。客户或经客户单独明确授权的提交人承担提交责任，并保留 FDA 确认文件。不可向本服务交付账户密码或多因素验证密钥。

## 3. 2026 program edition / 2026 年度

For the 2026 biennial renewal, FDA's published period is **October 1 through December 31, 2026**. New registrations, changes of ownership, prior renewals, exemptions, and qualified-facility attestations require their own factual determination. We do not guarantee that a facility must renew, that any submission will be accepted, or that a registration will remain valid.

2026 年续期时段为 **10 月 1 日至 12 月 31 日**。新注册、所有权变更、豁免及合格设施声明须逐项核实。

Official source: https://www.fda.gov/food/online-registration-food-facilities/food-facility-registration-user-guide-biennial-registration-renewal

## 5. Review and acceptance / 核对与验收

The Client receives the prepared brief, can identify inaccurate facts or incomplete materials, and expressly accepts or requests corrections under the signed scope. A delivery note or a generated checklist is not evidence that the customer accepted it. The service record is closed only on original customer acceptance. The provider's preparation does not change the FDA account or appoint a U.S. agent.

客户可提出更正；只有实际核对并接受交付成果才能完成服务，不得把清单生成视为已获 FDA 批准。

## 6. Parties' legal and data responsibilities / 责任与资料

The Client determines its own regulatory status and supplies true, current facility facts through approved channels. The Service Provider restricts its work to the agreed administrative assistance, uses only legitimately supplied information, protects client data, and refers legal or scientific determinations to the proper qualified professional. If foreign U.S.-agent services are needed, they require **a separate signed appointment, physical U.S. presence and responsibility for communications**.

The parties must independently settle governing law, confidentiality, liability, fees and return/correction remedies for the actual commercial engagement before signing. This template is **not an executed contract**.
